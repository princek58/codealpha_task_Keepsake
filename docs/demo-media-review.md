# Demo Media Editorial Review

## Editorial Goals & Guidelines
Every photo and video in Keepsake's demo dataset must feel like an authentic, calm personal memory:
- **No Close-Up Faces**: People are shown only from behind, at a distance, as hands, or as silhouettes.
- **No Brand Logos / Watermarks**: Clean, clutter-free compositions without visible consumer branding.
- **Natural Color & Mood**: Earthy, warm tones adhering to Keepsake's palette (`paper`, `sand`, `stone`, `terracotta`, `teal`, `sage`, `mustard`).
- **Aspect Ratio Variety**: A balanced distribution of landscape (4:3, 16:9, 16:10), portrait (3:4, 2:3), and square (1:1).
- **Cultural Inclusivity**: A thoughtful blend of Indian scenes (Varanasi ghats, Diwali diyas, chai cups, tawa rotis, samosas) and global landscapes (Himalaya peaks, European cobblestone alleys, alpine lakes, Nordic cabins).
