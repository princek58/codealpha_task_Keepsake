How Keepsake works

Preserve what matters in three thoughtful steps.

01

Upload your media

Bring your full-resolution photos and videos. Keepsake automatically optimizes them and strips sensitive location metadata.

02

Create Moments & Collages

Arrange pictures into beautiful editorial collage layouts, timeline grids, or horizontal carousels with custom spacing and colors.

03

Share on your terms

Choose who sees each moment: just you, approved followers, or everyone. Share private albums via secure links without requiring a login.
